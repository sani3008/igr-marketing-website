# Growth Catalyst

You are a senior product designer, creative director, UX strategist, conversion-rate optimization specialist, SEO architect and frontend engineer.

Your task is to completely redesign and upgrade the existing IGR Marketing website at:

https://igrmarketing.in/

The goal is NOT to make a minor visual refresh.

The goal is to create a premium, modern, conversion-focused digital agency website that feels significantly better than the current IGR website and more premium, polished and engaging than the reference website below:

Reference website:
https://makesyncplus.com/

IMPORTANT:

Use Makesync Plus ONLY as a design/UX inspiration and benchmark.

DO NOT clone its website.
DO NOT copy its layout pixel-for-pixel.
DO NOT copy its text.
DO NOT copy its branding.
DO NOT copy its visual identity.
DO NOT make IGR look like a duplicate of Makesync Plus.

Instead, study what works well on the reference website — especially its portfolio presentation, service hierarchy, navigation, CTA structure, visual rhythm, project showcasing and agency positioning — and then create a substantially better and more original experience for IGR Marketing.
